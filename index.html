    About this map

    Using it

    • Polities are states and empires (who governs). Civilizations are peoples and shared cultural identities (who you belong to). Societies are affinity groups that hold de facto control as logistical networks, how the world is actually run: states as networks, dynasties, trade routes, banks and companies, alliances, orders, NGOs, drawn as routes (lines), hubs (dots) and areas, and coloured by sphere of influence. Biomes are climate and ecoregions.
    • Tap the −100 to +100 buttons above the slider to step the year (hold to keep going; arrow keys step by 1, with Shift by 10).
    • Drag the year slider or press play. Click any area for its Wikipedia summary, infobox and facts.
    • Zoom in to see the provinces inside big polities and local identities inside peoples.
    • In Societies, click a kind in the legend to hide or show it.
    • In Civilizations, a click lists every identity you stand inside; none sits above the others.
    • Edit opens alt-history worlds: rename, recolour, change the years of, or remove a polity, and save the world in your browser. Planned (placeholders): the left arrow on the timeline for deep time before 3400 BCE, and Tiles for the tile view.
