        About this map

        Using it

        • Polities are states and empires (who governs). Societies are peoples and shared identities (who you belong to). Biomes are climate and ecoregions.
        • Drag the year slider or press play. Click any area for its Wikipedia summary, infobox and facts.
        • Zoom in to see the provinces inside big polities and local identities inside peoples.
        • In Societies, a click lists every identity you stand inside; none sits above the others.
